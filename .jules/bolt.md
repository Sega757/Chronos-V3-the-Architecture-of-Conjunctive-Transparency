## 2024-03-24 - [N+1 & Sorting Optimization on Article Blocks]
**Learning:** In the SCCS ACE Platform architecture, the primary content delivery routes (`/api/articles/slug/:slug` and `/api/articles/:id/blocks`) rely heavily on fetching ordered subsets of blocks from the `article_blocks` table using `SELECT * FROM article_blocks WHERE article_id = $1 ORDER BY position ASC`. While Postgres uses foreign keys to maintain integrity, it does NOT automatically index them. Without a composite index covering both the foreign key (`article_id`) and the sort column (`position`), this query forces a sequential scan across potentially millions of blocks followed by an in-memory sort.
**Action:** Always add composite indexes `(foreign_key_id, sort_column ASC)` to child tables that are frequently queried as ordered collections bound to a parent entity, rather than just indexing the foreign key alone.
